## Add new contact

Click the add new button (desktop) / plus sign button (mobile) to open the add new contact form, and enter the Identity, Name, Phone and Note for the new contact and click save.

**Desktop**

<img src="./assets/20260706_110536_contact-add-new.png" width="500"/>

![Watch video](./assets/add-new.webm)

**Mobile**

<img src="./assets/20260706_110738_contact-mobile-addnew.png" width="200" />

## Edit contact

Click on the contact name on list to open the edit contact form, on Mobile click the contact name on the list to view the contact details and click the pencil icon on top toolbar to open the edit form.

**Desktop**

<img src="./assets/contact-edit.png" width="500" />

Click the save button to save the changes

![Watch video](./assets/edit-contact.webm)

**Mobile**

<img src="./assets/contact-mobile-view.png" width="200" />

<img src="./assets/contact-mobile-edit.png" width="200" height="365" />

Click the check button to save the changes
